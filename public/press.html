SecretScan, in plain facts.

SecretScan is an iPhone and iPad privacy utility for finding passwords, recovery codes, API keys, IDs, payment details, and other sensitive text left in screenshots and photos.

View on the App StoreContact the developer

Facts last verified September 2, 2026. Press contact: Dmytro Polianskyi.

One-sentence description

Find sensitive information forgotten in your camera roll—without uploading the photos for analysis.

SecretScan uses Apple's on-device Vision framework for OCR, applies deterministic pattern checks locally, and presents likely findings for human review in a locked vault.

What it does

A focused local audit

  • Checks screenshots and eligible text-heavy photos
  • Looks for likely credentials, recovery material, financial details, IDs, and private text
  • Supports review, redacted-copy saving, and Photos deletion requests
  • Explains that exposed credentials should be rotated or revoked

What it is not

Deliberate boundaries

  • Not a password manager, antivirus, cloud vault, or identity-theft service
  • Not a guarantee that every sensitive image will be found
  • Not a replacement for human review
  • Not able to revoke a credential merely by deleting its image

Privacy model

Local Photos analysis with narrowly bounded product metrics.

Photos and extracted OCR text are not uploaded to SecretScan servers. No SecretScan account is required. Release builds may send one-time product-validation milestones through Firebase Analytics, but those custom events exclude Photos, OCR text, finding details, categories, counts, Photos identifiers, and account identifiers. SecretScan does not use the data for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.
