Three deliberate steps

Find it. Review it. Fix it.

01

Scan privately

On-device OCR checks text-heavy images without uploading your photos or extracted text to SecretScan servers.

02

Review the evidence

Risk-ranked findings help you inspect likely secrets. You stay in control because pattern matching can produce false positives.

03

Reduce the exposure

Save a redacted copy or delete the original. If a credential was exposed, rotate or revoke it as well.
